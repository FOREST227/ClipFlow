## Design Context

### Users
Mac users who repeatedly copy text, code, links and images between apps. The main job is to find and reuse a recent item quickly while keeping sensitive content controlled.

### Brand Personality
Native, compact, reassuring. Follow the established positioning in docs/clipflow-design.md: Spotlight, Control Center and Quick Look rather than a dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing macOS typography, light/dark appearance and category accents. The user prefers the original richer visual identity: warm-to-blue gradients, brand icons, status summaries, colorful category cards and content labels. Keep the newer page structure and scrolling behavior while restoring these elements at compact sizes. Avoid stripping the interface to plain text and monochrome controls.

Confirmed window widths: library and settings use 500 points; menu-bar popover and quick-paste HUD use 360 points. Keep these two width groups rather than making every surface equally wide.

History and settings share one 500-point main window with a persistent top page switcher: Settings on the left, Clipboard History on the right. Keep the general/privacy/sync groups inside settings. Page switching preserves settings drafts and history filters; never open a second settings window.

For individual settings controls, prefer simple, refined presentation: a compact input, subtle hover/recording highlight and one short hint. The user rejected decorative keyboard keycaps, pencil icons, extra dividers and repeated status text in the shortcut section.

### Design Principles
1. Balance content space with visual richness: compact brand headers and real status statistics, without oversized hero panels.
2. Keep navigation and primary actions stationary while only the content scrolls.
3. Use a 4-point spacing scale: 4/8 within related content, 12/16 between controls, 24 between sections.
4. Group settings by purpose and align labels, descriptions and controls consistently.
5. Preserve all existing actions, privacy safeguards and keyboard behavior.
